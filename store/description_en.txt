[Title]
zzWeatherPlus - Wind, Forecast, Sunrise/Sunset, Moon

[Description]
A data field that shows wind direction and speed relative to your riding direction, a 12-hour weather forecast, today's sunrise and sunset, and the moon's phase and brightness.
Built with the TitilliumWeb font for better readability on the move.
A rebuilt version of zzWeather that no longer stops when your location can't be read.

- Wind and forecast need your phone paired over Bluetooth (Garmin Connect app).
  Sunrise/sunset and moon are calculated on the device, so they work without a phone.

[Wind]
- Wind speed in m/s, km/h, mph or ft/s (set in the app settings).
- Track up (your direction always points up) or north up with a heading arrow.
- The gap in the outer ring is where the wind comes in. (Imagine the wind blowing in through the gap.)
  Headwind is red, crosswind gray, tailwind blue.
- Heading from GPS track or from the compass. (With GPS selected, the compass is used while stopped.)

[Forecast]
- Steps through the next 12 hours, one hour every 2 seconds.
- The number is the forecast hour, shown with that hour's wind direction arrow and chance of rain (%).
- Hours after sunset use night icons.

[Sunrise / Sunset]
- Today's sunrise and sunset. Times that have already passed are dimmed.
- 12-hour, 24-hour or follow the device setting.

[Moon]
- Moon brightness (%) and moon age for night rides.
- The moon is drawn from its actual phase (mirrored in the southern hemisphere).

[Auto layout by field size]
- Small field: the item you choose
- Wide field: wind on the left + your item on the right
- Large field: wind, forecast, sun and moon all at once
- Or set "Always one item, large".

[Touch]
- On touchscreen models, tap the field to switch items.
  (In a wide field only the right side changes: forecast, sunrise/sunset, moon.)
- Can be turned off in settings.

[Location]
- Location permission is used to calculate sunrise and sunset.
- The last known location is saved on the device when the app starts and while you ride, so it works even if you start indoors or before GPS is ready.
- Location stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

[Supported devices]
Edge 540 / 550 / 840 / 850 / 1040 / 1050 / 1060

Change settings in the Garmin Connect IQ app or Garmin Express.
